Build 7's timer (clears `SpeedMenuOpen` when neither the button nor the menu has focus) handled moving onto a neighbouring button in the headless test, but not the path the pointer takes on Windows. ## Conventions

- Detect a changed playback speed with `Player.IsTempo`, never by comparing `Player.PlaySpeed` alone: during fast-forward/rewind it reports the fast-forward multiplier (2.00 = 2x FF).
